Policies & Frequently Asked Questions

How does In-Seat Butler food delivery work?

Add snacks, burgers, pizza, sweets, or combos to your Lounge Tray on the Food & Snacks page, enter your hall and seat/recliner reference, complete payment, and our lounge team delivers directly to your row.

Can I switch between main pages and jump straight to sections?

Yes. Use the three main links in the top bar (Cinema & Repertoire, Food & Snacks, Guest Reviews) and the sub-navigation strip right beneath them to jump to any section.

How do I join the Free Ticket Jokes & Laughs contest?

Visit Guest Reviews, scroll to Jokes Corner, submit your joke, and tap Laugh 😂 (1 laugh per joke) on entries you enjoy. The highest laugh total leads for the free movie ticket.
