CHRONOLOGY

HERITAGE TIMELINE

  1. 2018 — THE MANHATTAN INAUGURATION

    Opening of the Fifth Avenue location with 32 luxury recliner seats, establishing an intimate, welcoming standard for private movie screenings.

  2. 2021 — VIP GOLD MEMBERSHIP CHARTER

    Launch of private vault memberships and 24/7 concierge box office, granting patrons bespoke cellar allocations and chauffeur staging.

  3. 2023 — DUAL-LASER ACOUSTIC STANDARD

    Partnership with master cinema acousticians for zero-bleed auditoriums, pairing room-within-a-room isolation with calibrated 4K laser projection.

  4. 2026 — CINEMA LOCATION EXPANSION

    Flagships operating across Manhattan, Beverly Hills, London Mayfair, and Dubai DIFC under one unified architectural standard.
